# Portfolio identity revision

## Decision

Keep audition A's terminal and ASCII visual language, but make the page clearly
about Nilson as a person and professional. The work should establish credibility
for the person; the interface should not read like a product landing page selling
an abstract service.

## One-page surface spec

- **Audience:** recruiters, hiring teams, and collaborators considering Nilson
  for RPA, data analysis, BI, and automation work.
- **Primary actions:** understand Nilson's background, inspect selected projects,
  and contact him through LinkedIn or email.
- **Languages:** Portuguese and English, with a visible language switch and
  equivalent content in both languages.
- **Devices:** responsive desktop and mobile layouts.
- **Identity:** A's dark terminal/ASCII direction, with ASCII used as a supporting
  motif. A real portrait and human introduction lead the page; terminal styling
  must not obscure the person or the projects.
- **Assets:** use Nilson's own material only. The verified GitHub profile photo is
  a 200px square crop and looks too soft for the large, prominent portrait this
  direction needs. Request an original portrait from Nilson. Do not use stock or
  generate a substitute. The profile photo may be used only if Nilson explicitly
  chooses it and it is presented at a size that preserves its quality.
- **Motion:** retain the previously selected High tier for restrained data-lineage
  motion; honor `prefers-reduced-motion` and keep content readable without motion.
- **Delivery:** finished bilingual website plus a PDF export of the selected
  website design.

## Proposed opening copy

### Portuguese

**Nilson Lopes**

**Dados, BI e automação com contexto de operação.**

Sou Nilson. Minha trajetória passou pelo design, por redes e por operações antes
de chegar a dados e BI. Essa experiência me ajuda a entender o problema de quem
usa a informação e a construir análises, pipelines e automações que tornam o
trabalho mais confiável.

### English

**Nilson Lopes**

**Data, BI, and automation grounded in operations.**

I'm Nilson. My path moved through design, networking, and operations before data
and BI. That experience helps me understand the people using the information and
build analysis, pipelines, and automation that make their work more reliable.

The wording is based on Nilson's public profile README and should remain free of
unverified metrics, credentials, or employment claims. The introduction is a
proposal for review, not approved final copy.

## Page order

1. **Personal hero:** portrait, name, short first-person introduction, focus
   areas (RPA, data analysis, BI, automation), and clear project/contact actions.
2. **Selected work:** evidence-led case studies for Planning Intelligence
   (`website-analytics`), The Cog, and `df-design`. State what each project does,
   Nilson's contribution, and link to its source. Label the Planning Intelligence
   sample as sanitized with fictional organization data.
3. **How I got here:** a concise professional arc connecting design, networks,
   operations, and data/BI, without presenting customer service as an expertise.
4. **Capabilities:** concrete tools and work types supported by public evidence;
   prioritize RPA, data analysis, and automation over an exhaustive skills list.
5. **Contact:** LinkedIn and a direct email address only if Nilson provides or
   approves the address.

## Review gate

Before implementation, Nilson should approve this identity and one-page spec,
review the proposed introduction, and provide a suitable original portrait (or
explicitly approve the GitHub avatar for a small crop). After the assets are
confirmed, re-price the selected High motion tier and build the page. The final
site must be visually reviewed at desktop and mobile widths in both languages;
the PDF follows the approved rendered page.

## Explicit exclusions

- No stock imagery or generated likeness.
- No customer-service-operator positioning.
- No invented results, metrics, certifications, or claims of project ownership.
- No implementation of additional pages or surfaces in this revision.
